beside this because the rounds were not interleaved and the environment column was never populated.

## Reading it

- The `v0.6.0` set contains patch 22's meta guard, so in every `-sm tensor` row above the decode
  slots were **off**: those two rows are the whole set minus 22, and they match the earlier
  hand-built skip-22 run (+29.1%) to within 0.1%. `-sm layer` keeps both devices real, so 22 is live
  there and that is where the decode jump is.
- Decode was the target and decode is what moved. Prompt processing is flat to slightly better; the
  two small regressions (−0.65%, −0.49%) are ~10x their own σ but come from un-interleaved arms, so
  they are within what an ordering or thermal bias could produce. Do not quote them as a cost.
- Not recorded, and needed before these are final: arm order and interleaving, GPU temperature per
  round, `LLAMA_*` environment variables, driver version, `-ngl` (shown as `-1`), and any
  output-identity or perplexity check. `-npl` was not run, so nothing here distinguishes a speedup
  from a change in what the model computes.
