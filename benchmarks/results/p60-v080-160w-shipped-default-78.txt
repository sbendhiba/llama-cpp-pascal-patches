What the new default changes about the *answer*, not just the speed. The persistent halves are a
different arithmetic path, so two of the three reference prompts now differ from the 74-patch build:
  prompt                              74 patches        78 patches

The size of the shift is in benchmarks/results/p60-v080-160w-gdn-half.txt: mean KLD 0.0284 +- 0.0086,
median 0.0111, 99th percentile 0.354 per token, against the per-kernel path. This is the same property
`MEGA` has always had (turning it on also changed the text); it is now the default, so it is stated
here plainly. `GGML_CUDA_P60_MEGA=0` or `GGML_CUDA_P60_GDN_HALF=0` returns to the per-kernel path, and
`GGML_CUDA_P60_EXACT` is the bit-exact mode.
